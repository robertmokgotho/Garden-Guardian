About Us

Helping gardeners plan smarter and grow better

Who We Are

We are a passionate team of students and developers who believe in empowering local communities through smart gardening. Our platform is designed to solve practical problems that everyday gardeners face.

Our Mission

To make gardening more accessible and efficient by providing digital tools that help people manage space, water usage, and pest control in their home gardens.

Our Vision

We believe in a future where anyone, anywhere, can grow their own food — regardless of space, climate, or experience. Our platform helps make that a reality.

| Our Services |

🌿 Pest Control

Pests can wipe out entire crops. Our app includes features to help diagnose common pest issues and suggest cost-effective, eco-friendly solutions.

💧 Water Management

Gardeners often don't know how much water they need, or how much it will cost. We calculate both, helping users plan their water usage responsibly.

📏 Space & Cost Planning

Many gardens are small. Our space calculator helps gardeners plan out their available land efficiently and provides a cost estimate based on chosen plants and space requirements.

How Our App Helps

  • ✅ Pest Diagnosis Tool
  • ✅ Water Usage and Cost Calculator
  • ✅ Space and Garden Layout Planner
  • ✅ Easy-to-use interface with helpful tips
  • ✅ Educational guides for beginner gardeners
