Welcome to
Garden-Guardian

Garden-Guardian is a web application built to help gardeners make the most out of their space, manage water usage, and fight pests efficiently. Whether you're planting vegetables in your backyard or building a community garden, we've got you covered.

| Services |

🌿 Pest Control

Pests can wipe out entire crops. Our app includes features to help diagnose common pest issues and suggest cost-effective, eco-friendly solutions.

💧 Water Management

Gardeners often don't know how much water they need, or how much it will cost. We calculate both, helping users plan their water usage responsibly.

📏 Space & Cost Planning

Many gardens are small. Our space calculator helps gardeners plan out their available land efficiently and provides a cost estimate based on chosen plants and space requirements.
